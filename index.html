    Things to do anytime

    No schedule needed. Good for the mornings when everyone's up at 6 and you need somewhere to be by 9.

    Get outside

    • East Rock ParkTrails, a summit with views over the city and harbor, and plenty of room to run.
    • Lighthouse Point ParkBeach, the old lighthouse, and a historic carousel that runs in season.
    • Edgewood ParkA big neighborhood park with a playground, pond and paths for scooters and strollers.
    • Common GroundFarm animals and gardens on Springside Ave. Saturday Open Farm Days are free through November.
    • Wooster SquareA stroller loop under the trees, and famous cherry blossoms in April. Pizza is right there.

    Rainy day, indoors

    • Yale Peabody MuseumDinosaurs, dioramas and weekend story times. Free admission.
    • Connecticut Children's MuseumHands-on play for small kids on Wall St. Open Thursday afternoons, Friday and Saturday.
    • Yale Center for British ArtFree, calm, and hosts Make Time! family art afternoons on fourth Saturdays.
    • Yale University Art GalleryFree and stroller-friendly, with a lot of room to wander.
    • Your library branchEvery NHFPL branch has a children's room, and Stay & Play runs somewhere almost every weekday.

    Worth a short drive

    • Shoreline Trolley MuseumReal trolley rides in East Haven, seasonal. A hit with anyone who loves vehicles.
    • Connecticut's Beardsley ZooAbout half an hour away in Bridgeport, sized just right for little legs.
    • Hammonasset BeachLong boardwalk and a nature center in Madison. Lovely in fall with no crowds.

    Resources for moms

    Help with the big things and the everyday things, from pregnancy through the toddler years.

    If you're struggling right now

    National Maternal Mental Health Hotline 1-833-852-6262

    Call or text, 24/7, for pregnant and new moms. Free and in English and Spanish.

    Postpartum Support International 1-800-944-4773

    Helpline for postpartum depression and anxiety, plus free online support groups.

    Connecticut 2-1-1 Dial 2-1-1

    Connects you to local help with food, housing, utilities, childcare and diapers.

    Emergency 911

    For any immediate danger to you or your child.

    Pregnancy to 18 months

    New Haven Healthy Start

    Care coordination, doula services, lactation support, parenting classes and help enrolling in insurance. Call 203-777-7086.

    Learn about Healthy Start
    Food and nutrition

    WIC

    Healthy food, formula help and breastfeeding support for pregnant people and kids under 5. Yale New Haven Hospital runs a WIC office in the city.

    Find your WIC office
    Diapers

    The Diaper Bank of Connecticut

    Gives out free diapers through partner agencies across the state. Dial 2-1-1 to find the nearest pickup spot.

    Visit the Diaper Bank
    Health insurance

    HUSKY Health

    Connecticut's free and low-cost coverage for kids and for pregnant and postpartum moms.

    Check HUSKY eligibility
    Development questions

    Connecticut Birth to Three

    Free early-intervention evaluations if you have questions about how your baby or toddler is talking, moving or playing. You don't need a doctor's referral.

    Visit Birth to Three
    Paying for childcare

    Care 4 Kids

    The state's childcare subsidy program for working families.

    See if you qualify
    Free, every week

    New Haven Free Public Library

    Library cards are free for New Haven residents. Branches host Stay & Play, bilingual storytime and more.

    See library storytimes

    Privacy policy

    Effective September 23, 2026

    New Haven Moms (newhavenctmoms.com) is a free guide to events, places and resources for families in New Haven, Connecticut. We keep things simple: this site has no accounts, no sign-up forms, no advertising trackers and no analytics. This policy explains the little information that does change hands when you use the site or get in touch.

    Information we collect

    We don't collect personal information through the website itself. You can browse the calendar, things to do and resources without telling us who you are, and the site doesn't set cookies.

    If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. That includes event tips, sponsorship inquiries and general messages.

    How we use it

    • To reply to you.
    • To add events you tell us about to the calendar. We publish event details like the name, place, time, ages, price and the organizer's public link. We don't publish your personal email address or contact details unless you ask us to list them as the event's contact.
    • To talk with businesses and organizations about sponsorship.

    We don't sell, rent or trade your information, and we don't add you to a mailing list without asking.

    Services we rely on

    A few outside services help run the site, and they may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when your browser connects to them:

    • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may keep server logs, including visitor IP addresses, for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.
    • Google Fonts supplies the site's typefaces, so your browser requests them from Google. See Google's privacy policy.
    • Gmail handles email sent to newhavenmoms@gmail.com.

    The site also links to other websites, such as Instagram, Google Maps, event organizers and community resources. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we don't control.

    Children's privacy

    This site is written for parents and caregivers. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has sent us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

    Keeping and deleting your information

    We keep emails only as long as they're useful for replying, updating the calendar or an ongoing sponsorship conversation. To ask what we have from you, or to have it deleted, email newhavenmoms@gmail.com.

    Changes to this policy

    If we add something new, like a newsletter sign-up or visitor analytics, we'll update this policy first and change the date at the top.

    Contact

    Questions about privacy? Email newhavenmoms@gmail.com.
